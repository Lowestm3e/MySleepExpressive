## About

**SleepMaster** is an Android sleep-tracking application designed around Google's Material 3 Expressive design language.

With a focus on simplicity, useful sleep insights, and a polished user experience, SleepMaster helps you understand your sleep habits through clear statistics, sleep history, and personalised goals.

The interface combines expressive typography, dynamic colours, smooth animations, and modern Material components to create an experience that feels right at home on Android.

## Features

- **Sleep Tracking**: Record your bedtime, wake-up time, and total sleep duration.
- **Sleep Statistics**: View your sleep duration and monitor your nightly patterns.
- **Sleep History**: Review previous nights and explore your progress over time.
- **Sleep Goals**: Set a target sleep duration and work towards a consistent routine.
- **Sleep Score**: Get an overview of your recorded sleep using a simple scoring system.
- **Material 3 Expressive**: A modern interface with expressive components, shapes, typography, and motion.
- **Dynamic Colour**: Adapt the interface to supported system colour schemes.
- **Light and Dark Themes**: Choose the appearance that suits you.
- **Intuitive Interface**: Navigate your sleep information through a clean, easy-to-use layout.

## Design Philosophy

SleepMaster follows three main principles:

- **Clarity**: Make sleep information easy to understand at a glance.
- **Consistency**: Use familiar Android design patterns and intuitive navigation.
- **Expression**: Bring the interface to life through Material 3 Expressive components, colour, typography, and motion.

The result is a sleep tracker that keeps the focus on your sleep without making the experience feel clinical or cluttered.
